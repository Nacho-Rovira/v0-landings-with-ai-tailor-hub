Design Tokens Used

Component Tokens (Button Primary)

  • --button-primary-bg: var(--color-neutral-100) (background)
  • --button-primary-bg-hover: var(--color-neutral-200) (hover background)
  • --button-primary-bg-active: var(--color-neutral-300) (active background)
  • --button-primary-text: var(--color-neutral-600) (text color)
  • --button-primary-text-hover: var(--color-neutral-900) (hover text color)
  • --button-primary-text-active: var(--color-neutral-900) (active text color)
  • --button-primary-padding: var(--spacing-xs) (8px)
  • --button-primary-text-size: var(--text-size-xsmall) (14px)
  • --button-primary-font: var(--font-tech) (PP Neue Montreal Mono)

Primitive Tokens

  • spacing.xs: 8px (padding)
  • spacing.xsm: 10px (gap between elements)
  • spacing.m: 16px (button group gap)
  • radius.xs: 8px (border radius)
  • text.line.xsmall: 17px (line height)
